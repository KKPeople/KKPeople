About KKPeople.

Our mission is to empower organizations by placing the right people at the heart of every decision.

KKPeople is a boutique recruitment and talent acquisition firm built from the inside of the hiring table — not the outside looking in. With over 10 years of experience, we've placed hundreds of professionals and partnered with some of the region's most ambitious organizations.

We operate across six industries: banking, technology, hospitality, construction, gaming, and NGOs — throughout Jordan, the GCC, and the wider MENA region. That breadth gives us the market-specific insight that generalist agencies simply don't have.

KKPeople is not a traditional HR agency. We are a boutique talent partner. Whether you're a startup building your core team or an established organization filling a critical leadership gap, the goal is always the same: right people, right now.

Based in Amman, Jordan. Operating across the region. We don't deal in volume or shortcuts — we deal in quality, long-term partnerships, and placements that stick.

Meet the Founder

Karim is the founder of KKPeople and a recruitment professional with over 10 years of experience across some of the most competitive markets in the GCC & MENA region.

His career spans banking, technology, hospitality, construction, gaming, and the NGO sector — giving him a rare cross-industry perspective on what it really takes to attract, assess, and retain top talent.

Karim founded KKPeople to bridge the gap that most firms ignore: the space between what companies actually need and what the market typically provides. His approach is direct, data-informed, and deeply relationship-driven.

Boutique recruitment and talent acquisition for ambitious companies across the GCC & MENA region. We find the people who move your business forward.
